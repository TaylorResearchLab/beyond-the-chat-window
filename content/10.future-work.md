# Future work

The clearest extension is a prospective study with multiple principal investigators. In that design, no single participant would hold continuous project memory, adjudication authority, and access control at the same time, so disagreements could be routed among investigators through their assistants rather than through one mediator.

Such a study would need instrumentation that cannot be reconstructed retrospectively: exposure status recorded at the time of action, review as an append-only event rather than mutable state, assignment timestamps distinct from record creation, a rubric frozen before data exist, and a written dispute plan agreed before work begins. The same architecture could also be tested with multiple named sessions from one provider. The binding constraint in either design will be logging discipline, which decays when instrumentation adds friction to real work.
