# Conclusion

This paper provides a workflow and practical materials for organizing sustained scientific work across AI platforms: a reusable contract, implementation guide, templates, and a development-stage Skill. A retrospective case documents how shared records, artifact links, review, and handoffs were used and where they failed. Its lessons are incorporated as explicit operating practices while remaining distinct from evidence of general effectiveness. Researchers can use the materials to construct and test a project-specific implementation; adoption success, coordination burden, and comparative benefit remain questions for prospective evaluation.
