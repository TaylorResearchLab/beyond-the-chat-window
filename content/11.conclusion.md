# Conclusion

This paper provides a User-directed workflow for sustained scientific work across AI platforms and SCUTER, which packages its reusable protocol, implementation guide, and templates as a development-stage Skill and complete Markdown instructions. A retrospective case documents how shared records, artifact links, review, and handoffs were used and where they failed. Its lessons are incorporated as explicit operating practices while remaining distinct from evidence of general effectiveness. Researchers can use the materials to construct and test a project-specific implementation; adoption success, coordination burden, and comparative benefit remain questions for prospective evaluation.
