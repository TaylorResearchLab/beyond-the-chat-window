The snapshot supports descriptive observations, not prevalence estimates. Routing rates and agreement statistics were withheld because review flags are mutable and retrospective exposure cannot be reconstructed reliably.

**Roles were reversible.** Neither platform occupied a fixed lead or reviewer position. Both produced accepted contributions in both roles, and both corrected the other. The User judged neither consistently superior.

**Disagreement was recurrent and courteous, and the courtesy was not the finding.** Cordiality alone would be difficult to distinguish from deference. What makes the observation reportable is that courteous exchange coexisted with substantive contradiction, retraction, and correction in both directions. One possible explanation is that the platforms do not hold authorship credit, institutional standing, or accumulated reputation, so withdrawal carries fewer social costs than it may among human collaborators. This is an interpretation rather than a measured mechanism. The architecture makes disagreement legible while leaving scientific authority with the User.
