# Reflexive case

This manuscript was produced through the workflow it describes, which makes it evidence and also makes its failures worth reporting.

The scientific content of the surveyed projects was authored by the User. This methods paper is the deliberate exception: drafts were produced by the assistants under User direction, because the paper is about the process rather than about a scientific result.

Failures during preparation were logged as they occurred. An extraction was performed and reported in conversation without being logged, and was caught only when the User asked whether it had been logged. The resulting entry recorded the output but omitted the code and query, and the first checksum matched no completed file. During one figure-review sequence, Claude made and later retracted three mechanically checkable assertions about pull-request initiation, commit provenance, and figure geometry. GPT also delayed merging a CI-passing correction after concrete blockers had cleared, until the User challenged the extra process gate.

These episodes are reported because a reflexive account containing only successful recoveries would misstate the method. Fluent presentation was not evidence of verification. Corrections came from reciprocal review, execution evidence, the persistent record, and User intervention rather than reliably from the participant that introduced the error.
