## Manuscript production as an additional case

The manuscript was prepared with the workflow it describes. Its production record was not included in the frozen 728-record dataset and supplies separate examples of why explicit controls were needed.

An analysis was reported in chat without being logged. Its first log entry then omitted the code and query that produced it and contained a checksum calculated before the file was complete. In another sequence, three directly checkable claims were stated without verification. A build was described as having validated citations and links before every target had been opened; one public link failed, and one real source did not support its assigned claim. A merge was also delayed after the agreed review requirements had been met. The other platform or the User identified each problem and required correction.

A further inconsistency concerned context integration: the Introduction cited a human-AI taxonomy source while a separate reciprocal citation-audit record still described that source as unused. Both records existed, but their disagreement became visible only when the workstreams were reconciled.

These incidents connect specific failures to the proposed operating practices: retain the materials behind a result, compute checksums after completion, validate claim-source correspondence separately from the build, and reconcile current artifacts with review records. They do not constitute additional independent replications or a controlled test of those practices.
