## Governance

Coordination rules are stated in advance as a work contract rather than negotiated per dispute. The contract is analogous to a multi-investigator leadership plan: it names authority, evidence expectations, handoff rules, and a process for resolving differences before they arise. It was developed independently for this workflow.

Human leadership plans presume that each participant has institutional responsibilities, authorship interests, and professional consequences. The assistants do not hold these forms of standing. What transfers is the need to state who decides, what evidence controls, and what happens when participants do not converge. The Collaboration Log then records how each disagreement was actually resolved.

Two clauses were added during this work in response to observed failures. **Output custody:** every material output is logged, and code, inputs, and results are stored together, either inline or by reference to a durable location chosen by the User. A result recorded without the means of producing it does not satisfy the clause. **Read-back verification:** after any write that creates or relocates a record, the record is fetched again and checked on two independent points, that it has the intended parent and that its properties survived.
