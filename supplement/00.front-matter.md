## Evidence boundary for the implementation guide

The practical guidance in this supplement derives from one documented implementation with one human User and was not compared against alternative workflows. It is provided as a reproducible starting configuration rather than as a validated best practice. Adopters should adjust the workflow to their scientific risk, institutional policies, privacy requirements, and available software, and should prospectively record model, session, and exposure information when those variables matter to later interpretation.
